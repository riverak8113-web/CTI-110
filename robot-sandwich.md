
# Cooked Ham Salami And Cheese Sandwich

## Ingredients
- 2 Slices of Ham
- 1 Slice of Salami
- 1 Slice of Cheese
- 2 Slices of whole wheat bread
- Sandwich Grill

## Assumptions
- Ingredients already bought
- All ingredients are out and placed on the counter
- Sandwich Grill has been bought and user knows how to operate it
- Plate was originally bought
- Plate is already on the counter

## Instructions
1. Place 1 slice of whole wheat bread on the plate
2. Place a slice of Ham on top of the slice of bread
3. Place a slice of salami on top of the slice of Ham
4. Place another slice of Ham on top of the slice of Salami so that the Salami is between the slices of Ham
5. Place a slice of Cheese on top of the entire combination
6. Place another slice of whole wheat bread on top of the entire combination afterwards
7. Heat up the sandwich grill for approximately 5 minutes
8. Grab the whole combination and place it in the grill, then close the lid
9. Wait approximately 10 minutes

## Done When
- Cheese is melted
- Bread is crispy/crunchy
